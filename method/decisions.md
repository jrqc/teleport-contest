review of the Stage 1 design (`method/design.md` section 12.2). D8 onward were taken during
Stages 2 and 3. "Owner" marks a decision the owner made or approved; "Rules" marks one the
implementation agent took within the standing rules of `CLAUDE.md` (no special cases, root
causes, generated code never edited by hand). Sources are the commits, `method/postmortem.md`,
